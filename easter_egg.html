My Erdős number is 4, because
I co-authored a paper with Eddie Dekel,
who co-authored a paper with Michal Feldman,
who co-authored a paper with Noga Alon,
who co-authored a paper with Paul Erdős.*

My doctoral advisor was Eddie Dekel,
whose doctoral advisor was Jerry Green,
whose doctoral advisor was Lionel McKenzie,
whose doctoral advisor was John Hicks.**

At Nuffield College (in the Roy Harrod fellowship),
I succeeded Ian Jewitt,
who succeeded Richard Spady,
who succeeded John Vickers.

Which was the son of…



* This proves that my Erdős number is ≤4. Proof that it is ≥4: none of my co-authors appear on the list of people with Erdős number ≤2.

** Hicks was McKenzie's doctoral advisor at Oxford. However, McKenzie left Oxford in 1948 without having earned his doctorate, and began his career. Much later (1956), Oskar Morgenstern arranged for Princeton to award McKenzie a doctorate on the basis of his existing papers.
